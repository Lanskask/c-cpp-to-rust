## The four known items

1. **(a) `detach_path` case-insensitive detach.** The behaviour is faithful and the Judge already covers it (corpus
   `replace /FOO` on `PATCH_DOC` under `cs=1`). It is still a quirk without a row and without a marker. Row 6 gives the
   exact Q27 text; add `// QUIRK(port): Q27` above the object branch of `detach_path_in`.
2. **(b) NULL `valuestring` in a patch member.** It cannot stay as written. Two of the five cases are defined in C:
   `test` with a NULL `path` gives status 1 and `copy` with a NULL `from` gives status 5, and Rust differs on both
   (row 3, measured). The fix is to pass the `Option` through to `get_item_from_pointer`, which already returns `None`
   for `None` as C does. The other three cases are NULL dereferences in C (`op`, the `path` of a non-`test` op, the
   `from` of `move`). They need the D22 row (row 8). The rule proposed there reuses C's own NULL handling, so Rust
   picks no new semantics. The current choice of reading NULL as `""` turns a `remove` into "overwrite the root",
   which is the worst choice available.
3. **(c) `#![allow(non_snake_case)]`.** Row 11. The cheapest fix is two item-level allows plus one §5 sentence, with
   the gate's allow check updated to accept exactly those two attributes. If the governor prefers no allow at all,
   `#[path]` modules with snake-case names do it at the cost of the `use` lines in the adapter and in
   `cJSON_Utils.rs`.
4. **(d) AllocHooks events in Utils and print.** For print, the Judge's harness already compares `Print` and
   `PrintBuffered(8)` fail-sweeps and counts. `PrintPreallocated` allocates nothing. For Utils, I measured the event
   traces and fail-the-Nth sweeps against C: 6000 random pairs, 216,944 lines, identical (row 15). The two
   divergences are both on D2 paths under a failing hook (rows 1 and 2). That is why the sweep proposed in row 15
   keeps D2 sites to counting only.
